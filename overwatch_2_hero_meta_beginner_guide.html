Top 6 Quick Play & Beginner Heroes By Role

Forgiving kits, intuitive mechanics, and strong self-reliance for players learning the game or playing casual queues.

New Player Recommended

Tank: Quick Play & Beginners

6 Heroes

Straightforward defense, clear feedback, and high durability
Hero Complexity Why It's Great for Beginners Enjoyment Excels At Struggles With
Reinhardt Low Clear frontline identity. Massive shield protects allies effortlessly; hammer swings hit in wide arcs without precision aim. Very High Satisfying hammer Choke dominance, pushing through payload bottlenecks, Earthshatter team stuns. Getting kited on open maps; long-range crossfires.
Roadhog Low–Med Massive self-heal with Take a Breather minimizes dependency on support teammates in solo queue. High Addictive hook Instantly punishing bad enemy positioning; independent survival. Anti-heal grenade (Ana); acts as an Ultimate battery for enemies.
Orisa Low Fortify makes her immune to stuns and crowd control, forgiving positioning mistakes. Medium–High Immovable wall Absorbing heavy frontline focus fire; pushing back aggressive tanks. Peeling for backlines; high-mobility dive comps bypassing her.
Sigma Medium Mobile shield can be pushed forward or recalled instantly; primary fire bounces around corners. High Controlled rhythm Teaches sightline awareness, shield management, and poke control. Fast close-quarters brawlers rushing inside his barrier.
Mauga Low Simple dual chain guns (ignite then crit). Extremely familiar to traditional FPS players. High Continuous fire Mowing down large enemy tanks; charging through crowds unstoppable. Ana's anti-heal grenade and Defense Matrix eating his lifesteal.
Zarya Medium Intuitive feedback loop: bubble yourself or ally when taking damage to supercharge your laser beam. High Max energy beam Melting mobile heroes with 100% beam; cleansing debuffs and burns. Disciplined teams that hold fire, keeping her energy bar near zero.
